# Top 10 ARPG-Inspired MUD Commands for Solo Play (Telnet Interface)

This list presents ten commands tailored for solo players seeking an ARPG-like experience within MUD environments. These commands focus on real-time combat, efficient resource management, and streamlined exploration, all accessible via a Telnet interface.

---

## 1. `autoattack`

- **Purpose**: Initiates continuous attacks on a target without repeated input.
- **Usage**: `autoattack [target]`
- **Benefit**: Emulates ARPG auto-attack mechanics, reducing repetitive keystrokes.

---

## 2. `quickcast`

- **Purpose**: Casts a pre-assigned spell or ability instantly.
- **Usage**: `quickcast [spell/ability]`
- **Benefit**: Facilitates rapid skill deployment, akin to hotkeys in ARPGs.

---

## 3. `dash`

- **Purpose**: Performs a swift movement to evade or close distance.
- **Usage**: `dash [direction]`
- **Benefit**: Introduces dynamic movement options, enhancing combat fluidity.

---

## 4. `lootall`

- **Purpose**: Collects all loot from a defeated enemy or area.
- **Usage**: `lootall`
- **Benefit**: Streamlines the looting process, similar to ARPG auto-loot features.

---

## 5. `equipbest`

- **Purpose**: Automatically equips the best available gear.
- **Usage**: `equipbest`
- **Benefit**: Optimizes gear management, reducing downtime between battles.

---

## 6. `useflask`

- **Purpose**: Consumes a health or mana flask.
- **Usage**: `useflask [health/mana]`
- **Benefit**: Mimics ARPG potion systems for quick resource recovery.

---

## 7. `map`

- **Purpose**: Displays the current area map.
- **Usage**: `map`
- **Benefit**: Assists in navigation, reflecting ARPG minimap functionalities.

---

## 8. `bind`

- **Purpose**: Sets a return point or "home" location.
- **Usage**: `bind [location]`
- **Benefit**: Allows quick travel, similar to town portals in ARPGs.

---

## 9. `combo`

- **Purpose**: Executes a sequence of attacks or abilities.
- **Usage**: `combo [sequence_name]`
- **Benefit**: Enables complex attack patterns, enhancing combat depth.

---

## 10. `inspect`

- **Purpose**: Provides detailed information about an enemy or item.
- **Usage**: `inspect [target/item]`
- **Benefit**: Informs strategic decisions, akin to ARPG stat inspection.

---

These commands aim to bridge the gap between traditional MUD gameplay and the fast-paced, responsive mechanics of ARPGs, offering solo players an enriched experience through the Telnet interface.
